About this map✕

    
You sit at the center. Everyone you add orbits you — color shows how you know them, and distance shows how close you are, from your Inner Circle out to Acquaintances.

    
The rings echo anthropologist Robin Dunbar's research on the layered structure of human social groups — roughly 5 intimate confidants, 15 close friends, 50 real friendships, and about 150 relationships we can meaningfully maintain at once.

    
Drag anyone to reposition them. Tap to open their profile. Link two people who know each other — mark partners and parent/child ties and the family tree draws itself, kids branching down from the couple that raised them.

    
Name a group for anything that clusters people — a school, a hometown, a team — and it becomes its own small orbit. Everything drifts gently, like a real night sky, and everything stays on this device.
